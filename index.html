Square exports at 1080 × 1080 px, portrait at 1080 × 1440 px and story at 1080 × 1920 px — full resolution for social feeds, WhatsApp and A5/A4 flyer printing.

The Android app is fully offline — design and export with no connection at all. This web builder also designs and exports entirely on your device; your wording is never uploaded.
